Paid Sick Days (Ontario)

1. Who this applies to. All employees working in Ontario.

2. Entitlement. You have 3 paid sick days each calendar year after two weeks of employment, plus 2 additional company-paid days.

3. How to use them. Tell your manager as early as you can. No doctor's note is needed for the first three days.

4. Carry-over. Unused sick days do not carry over to the next year.
